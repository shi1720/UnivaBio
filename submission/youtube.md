## Description

Anita is home from hospital, but one result is still pending. Looplight turns the next steps already written in discharge notes into a source-grounded plan people can review and track.

This narrated demo shows actual interactions with the hosted prototype: importing fictional discharge instructions, reviewing the exact source and missing details, recovering an omitted task, naming the person tracking it, distinguishing result receipt from reported clinician review, and recording the follow-up history. It also shows the visit brief, calendar export, and saved care space.

Try the prototype: https://looplight-care.web.app

Disclosure: AI-generated narration, not Shivam Gupta's voice. Anita, Maya, and the clinical details are fictional. This is a working prototype evaluated with synthetic examples, not clinical validation. It does not diagnose, interpret results, or demonstrate a proven health benefit. The $149 pricing shown is a hypothesis. Reported clinician review and completion are user-entered records, not independent clinical verification.
